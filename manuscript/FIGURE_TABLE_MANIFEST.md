## Supplement rule

If a result is necessary to verify a main-text claim but is not necessary to understand
that claim on first reading, it belongs in the supplement.

Likely supplementary material includes:

- full cell estimates;
- model/system/variant-stratified estimates;
- complete matched-pair tables;
- rank diagnostics;
- full validation confusion matrices;
- detailed omission breakdowns;
- technical provenance and deviation tables.
